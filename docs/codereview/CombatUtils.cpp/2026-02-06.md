## 6. Gaps & Bugs

### BUG-1: Atan2 Coordinate Inversion
*   **Priority**: 🟠 **High** | **Impact**: Input Precision
*   **Description**: `VectorToInputDirection` uses `Atan2(X, Y)`. In Unreal's 2D coordinate space convention, this can lead to 90-degree rotational drift if the caller expects standard Cartesian `Atan2(Y, X)`.
*   **Fix**:
    *   **Rationale**: Standardize on Unreal's `LookAt` math.
    *   **Action**: Change to `FMath::Atan2(InputVector.Y, InputVector.X)` to align with Unreal's world-to-local projection.

### GAP-1: Mesh-Offset Drift
*   **Priority**: 🟡 **Medium** | **Impact**: Targeting
*   **Description**: `GetMeshCompensatedRotation` uses `Fmod` for normalization, which is less precise than `FRotator::GetNormalized()` or `FMath::UnwindDegrees`.
*   **Fix**: Replace manual normalization with `ActorRotation.Normalize()`.

## 7. Action Items
