## 6. Gaps & Bugs

### GAP-1: `bInComboBlend` and `BlendTransitionEndTime` deprecated but still exposed.
*   **Priority**: 🟠 **High** | **Impact**: Code Clarity / Designer Confusion
*   **Description**: The flags `bInComboBlend` and `BlendTransitionEndTime` are marked as deprecated, but they are still exposed as `UPROPERTY` with `VisibleAnywhere` and `BlueprintReadOnly`. This means they are visible to Blueprint designers and can be misleading.
*   **Fix**: While keeping them for backward compatibility in serialization is necessary, they should ideally be hidden from the editor (e.g., `meta=(DeprecatedProperty, Hidden)`) or moved to a private block to reduce confusion.
