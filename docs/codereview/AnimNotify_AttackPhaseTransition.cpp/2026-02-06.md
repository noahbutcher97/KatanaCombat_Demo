## 6. Gaps & Bugs (Extended)

### BUG-2: Notify Skipping
*   **Priority**: 🟠 **High** | **Impact**: Gameplay Stability
*   **Description**: If the notify is at the extreme end of a montage section, Unreal may skip it during high-load frames or early blending. This leaves the `CombatComponent` stuck in the previous phase (e.g., Infinite Active phase).
*   **Fix**:
    *   **Rationale**: Software-side "Safety Catch."
    *   **Action**: `CombatComponent` should have a "ExpectedPhaseEndTime" and auto-transition if the montage passes it without a notify firing.
