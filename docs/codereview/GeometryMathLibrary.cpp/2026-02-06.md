## 6. Gaps & Bugs

### GAP-1: Redundant Normalization
*   **Priority**: Low | **Impact**: Performance
*   **Description**: Many methods call `.GetSafeNormal()` multiple times on the same vector during a single calculation.
*   **Fix**: Cache the normalized vector at the start of the function.

### GAP-2: Missing 2D-Only Variants
*   **Priority**: 🟡 **Medium** | **Impact**: Accuracy
*   **Description**: Targeting logic often needs to ignore the Z-axis (height) for distance checks to prevent enemies on higher ledges from being "closer" than enemies on the same level.
*   **Fix**: Implement `DistSquared2D` wrappers for all distance checks.
